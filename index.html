// what i do

Beyond the demo

Most GenAI projects die between the notebook and production. My work is the part that comes after: making retrieval accurate, answers traceable, and the whole thing stable under real load.

Enterprise RAG architecture

Multi-layer retrieval: semantic and hybrid search, metadata filtering and re-ranking. Knowledge ingestion pipelines that parse, normalize, index and version documents so answers stay traceable to a source.

Reliability & evaluation

Hallucination mitigation, controlled context injection and business-rule alignment. Evaluation frameworks, observability and feedback loops, so answer quality is something you measure instead of something you hope for.

AI backend engineering

API design and microservice-oriented deployments, containerized infrastructure and rollout strategy, performance tuning and scalability planning. The unglamorous layer that decides whether the model ever reaches a user.

// about me

AI engineer,
electromechanical technician first

I'm Constantino Ferrucci, a Generative AI engineer based in Montevideo, Uruguay. I design, deploy and optimize production-grade GenAI systems — the kind that have to keep answering correctly on a Tuesday afternoon, not just in a demo.

I'm currently a Senior GenAI Engineer at Apple, via Tata Consultancy Services, architecting end-to-end systems that connect large language models to structured and unstructured data sources, enterprise platforms and service-oriented backends — including agent-based workflows built on LangChain, LangGraph and MCP.

In parallel I teach as an Auxiliar de Primera in Computer Vision at the Universidad Nacional de Rosario, where I also taught NLP. Before that I spent three years at Wiener lab. building AI systems for clinical laboratory workflows — document computer vision, data pipelines and internal assistants.

My path started in electromechanics, not computer science. That detour is the reason I care more about whether a system holds up under load than about whether the architecture diagram is elegant.

fastapi-fastmcp-autogen

Write your logic once, get both a REST API and a set of MCP tools. No duplicated handlers, in either direction.

  • MCP → FastAPI: inspects registered tools, builds Pydantic models dynamically and registers the matching POST endpoints
  • FastAPI → MCP: walks the defined routes, extracts their Pydantic models and exposes them as MCP tools
  • Swagger docs come along for free; near-zero configuration either way

  • Scored with NDCG@10, precision, recall and F1 — students train their own models against it
